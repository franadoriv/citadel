Parties are authenticated, local account groups used to queue an indivisible
matchmaker ticket. A party has one leader and up to eight members. The leader
invites accounts; an invited account accepts before it becomes a member. A party
ticket represents every connected member, so it either forms as a whole or not at
all. Each member later receives and accepts their own matchmaker handoff.

All methods below use the generic realtime RPC envelope. Guests receive
`"authentication required"`. Party state is local to one node and is cleared on
restart; cross-node party ownership is not implemented.

## Response shape

Successful create, invite, accept, promote, remove, and status calls return:

```json
{
  "party_id": "opaque-id",
  "leader_user_id": "alice",
  "members": ["alice", "bob"],
  "invitations": []
}
```

## `party.create`

**Payload:** `{}`. **Returns:** the response shape above with the authenticated
caller as sole leader/member. An account may belong to one active party only.

## `party.invite`

**Payload:** `{"party_id":"…","target_user_id":"bob"}`.

**Returns:** the updated party snapshot. Only the leader may invite; the target
must not already be a member of another party. The maximum of eight includes
outstanding invitations.

## `party.accept`

**Payload:** `{"party_id":"…"}`.

**Returns:** the updated party snapshot. Only the invited authenticated account
can accept. A random party id alone is not enough without an outstanding invite.

## `party.leave`

**Payload:** `{"party_id":"…"}`. **Returns:** `{"left":true}`.

A normal member leaves immediately. When the leader leaves, the local party is
closed rather than silently transferring leadership; use `party.promote` first
when another member should own it.

## `party.promote`

**Payload:** `{"party_id":"…","target_user_id":"bob"}`.

**Returns:** the updated party snapshot. Only the current leader can promote an
existing member.

## `party.remove`

**Payload:** `{"party_id":"…","target_user_id":"bob"}`.

**Returns:** the updated party snapshot. Only the leader can remove a non-leader
member. A leader cannot remove itself; use `party.close` or promote first.

## `party.close`

**Payload:** `{"party_id":"…"}`. **Returns:** `{"closed":true}`.

Only the leader can close a party. Closing clears its invitations and lets every
former member create or join another party.

## `party.status`

**Payload:** `{"party_id":"…"}`.

**Returns:** the response shape above. Only a member or an invited account can
read a party snapshot.

## Queueing the party

The leader adds `party_id` to [`matchmaker.add`](/reference/client-sdk/matchmaker/#matchmakeradd):

```json
{
  "party_id": "opaque-id",
  "min_count": 4,
  "max_count": 4,
  "ttl_ms": 30000
}
```

Every member must be currently connected. While the ticket is queued, party
membership and invitations are frozen; cancel the ticket before changing them.
The matchmaker counts all members toward the cohort size and never forms a subset
of the party. Each member gets a distinct, account-bound `KIND_MATCHMAKER_MATCHED`
token and may recover it through `matchmaker.status` after reconnecting.
